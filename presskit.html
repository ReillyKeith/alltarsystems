Short Description

Chaotic isometric crash-for-cash driving. Smash fast, trigger slow-mo Crash Mode, aftertouch your burning wreck into the city's most expensive real estate, and get the itemised invoice. The joke is always the price tag.

Key Features

  • Slow-Mo Crash Mode & Aftertouch: Impact above threshold speed to trigger time dilation and steer your flaming wreck directly into luxury showrooms and fuel depots.
  • The Itemised Invoice: Watch damage climb in real-time with satirical price tags on hydrants, traffic, police cruisers, and multi-million dollar corporate glass towers.
  • Rising City Retaliation: 5-Star Wanted escalation dispatches relentless police cruisers and aggressive ambulances.
  • 24-Car Fleet & Progression: Purchase vehicles ranging from rusty $0 beaters to a 6-wheel executive limo with custom ramp aerodynamics.
  • Pure Arcade Performance: True 2:1 isometric projection, high-contrast night neon, dynamic day/night cycles, locked 60+ FPS on PC and Steam Deck.
  • High-Octane Punk Audio: Procedural and authored adrenaline rock soundtrack matching the pace of destruction.

About Alltar Systems

Alltar Systems is an independent game studio focused on crisp arcade physics, tactile destruction feedback, and satirical dark comedy game loops.
