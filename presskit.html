Description

CARNAGE is a fast, chaotic isometric arcade driving game made by indie developer Keith at Alltar Systems. Inspired by the crash modes and top-down racers of the late 90s and early 2000s, players drive full throttle into busy city streets, trigger slow-motion Crash Mode on collision, and steer their burning chassis to wipe out expensive landmarks.

Every smashed streetlight, police car, and glass building generates an instant insurance claim against Mutual Assurance Corp. The bigger the total bill, the more cash players earn to unlock heavier and faster cars.

Key Features

  • Slow-Mo Crash Mode: Collisions trigger slow motion. Guide your airborne wreckage through intersections using direct aftertouch steering.
  • Priced Destruction: From $100 fire hydrants to the $2,500,000 Mutual Assurance glass atrium and the $2,800,000 Barn Inferno, every broken object adds to the bill.
  • 6 Destruction Maps: Play through 6 handcrafted disaster maps: Downtown, The Coast, The Countryside, Drive-Thru Safari, Frozen Lake, and Snagging List Construction Site.
  • 20+ Unlockable Vehicles: Progress through 5 tiers of vehicles, from rusty sedans to taxis, hearses, and limos.
  • Free Playable Demo: Standalone demo with Downtown and The Beater available on Steam and itch.io (Windows exe and macOS dmg).
