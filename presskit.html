Description

CARNAGE is a chaotic isometric crash-for-cash driving game inspired by the legendary crash modes of the 2000s arcade golden era. Players floor the throttle into dense traffic, trigger slow-motion Crash Mode upon impact, and steer their burning chassis using direct aftertouch physics to wipe out multi-million dollar landmarks.

Every shattered window, pancaked ambulance, and pulverized traffic light produces an instant cash claim against Mutual Assurance Corp. The bigger the disaster invoice, the more funds players unlock to expand their fleet with heavier and more satirical destruction vehicles.

Key Features

  • The Crash is the Finale: High-speed collisions trigger slow-mo Crash Mode. Guide your spinning, airborne wreckage through intersections with aftertouch physics.
  • Everything Has a Price Tag: From $100 fire hydrants to the $2,500,000 Mutual Assurance glass lobby and the $2,800,000 Barn Inferno, every collision itemizes an instant cash payout.
  • Escalating City Response: Cause mayhem to build your 5-Star Wanted rating. Squad cars establish roadblocks, and ambulances rush in to respond.
  • 20+ Unlockable Satirical Vehicles: Progress through 5 tiers of corporate excess — from rusty sedans to commercial taxis, hearse battering rams, and six-wheel limousines.
  • 3 Launch Disaster Areas + 5 Roadmap Expansions: Launching with Downtown, The Coast, and The Countryside, with Safari Park, Frozen Lake, and Casino expansions planned.
