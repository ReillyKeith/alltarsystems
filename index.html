HOW THE CARNAGE WORKS

A lightning-fast, highly replayable arcade loop designed around pure destruction physics.

01

SPEED & IMPACT

Floor the throttle into heavy commuter traffic, build speed multipliers, dodge obstacles, and hit oncoming lanes at maximum velocity.

02

SLOW-MO CRASH MODE

On impact, time slows to a crawl. Use direct Aftertouch Steering to guide your burning chassis into fuel tankers, squad cars, and landmark buildings.

Watch your repair invoice tick up in real time. Every approved insurance settlement funds your garage with heavier, faster, and more destructive vehicles.
