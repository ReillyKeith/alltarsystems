## 📲 Channel-by-Channel Setup & Strategy

### 1. TikTok & YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels (Top Priority for Virality)
* **Format**: Vertical (9:16), 10–18 seconds long.
* **Sound**: Fast synthwave, metal, or trending punchy sounds.
* **Three Winning Video Concepts**:
  1. **Concept A (The Bill)**: Show full-throttle driving, slow-mo glass shattering into the lobby, and freeze-frame on the itemized receipt tallying up millions.
  2. **Concept B (The Close Call)**: Threading the needle through traffic at 120 mph before obliterating a police cruiser.
  3. **Concept C (Dev Story)**: Screen recording of you testing the demo on Mac / Steam Deck with text: *"Spent 6 months building a game where you just smash cars for insurance payouts. Free demo is live on itch!"*
* **Hashtags**: `#indiegame #gamedev #gaming #steamdeck #gamer #itchio #crash #arcade`
