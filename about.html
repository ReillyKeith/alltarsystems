🇮🇪 THE INDIE STORY

Hi, I'm Keith — the solo developer behind Alltar Systems, based in Ireland.

I grew up playing games during the golden era of arcade and console gaming in the late 1990s and early 2000s. It was a magical time when games didn't take themselves too seriously — they were loud, fast, unapologetically silly, and built around immediate, addictive gameplay loops.

Remember the unhinged joy of Burnout's Crash Mode, the top-down mayhem of classic GTA 1 & 2, the crunch of Destruction Derby, and the pure kinetic energy of Crazy Taxi? That spirit is what drives everything we build at Alltar Systems.

🔥 THE ALLTAR CREED

"No 40-minute unskippable tutorials. No Battle Passes. No microtransactions. Just hit SPACE, floor the throttle, crash into Mutual Assurance HQ, and laugh at the itemised invoice."

CARNAGE was born from a simple question: What if the crash wasn't the failure state, but the entire point of the game? Every lamppost, police cruiser, tractor, superyacht, and glass atrium has a real-time dollar tag attached to it. Your job is simple: cause the biggest insurance catastrophe in municipal history and bill the adjusters for every cent.

⚡ OUR CORE PRINCIPLES

Every design decision in our games is guided by three straightforward rules:

⏱️

BOOT-TO-PLAY IN 2 SECONDS

You bought a game to play it. Press Space anywhere in the menus and you're instantly in a round. Zero friction, zero fluff.

💥

PHYSICS & CHAOS FIRST

Real-time mass calculations, slow-motion aftertouch steering, secondary chain explosions, and responsive impact shockwaves.

🎸

90s / 2000s ATTITUDE

Pounding punk-rock guitar riffs, high-contrast arcade neons, cheeky insurance satire, and honest, fair-priced indie gaming ($3.99 – $4.99).
